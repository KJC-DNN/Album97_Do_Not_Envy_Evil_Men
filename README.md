# Album 97 — Do Not Envy Evil Men

Eight bilingual songs from Proverbs 24–31. Be not envious of evil men. A word fitly spoken is like apples of gold. Iron sharpens iron. The wicked flee when no man pursues. Where there is no vision the people perish. Every word of God is pure. Who can find a virtuous woman. Tamil original plus English cinematic companion. Last album of this Proverbs run.
